About

Hi, I'm Happy.

Yes, that's really my name. I'm Ms. Happy Pascal Mmila — an Airbnb coach who helps people turn properties into profitable, sustainable income.

With deep knowledge of the short-term rental industry, I guide hosts step by step — from listing creation and pricing strategy to guest experience and scaling for long-term success.

Through practical training, mentorship and proven techniques, I help hosts maximise bookings, increase revenue and stand out in a competitive market. Whether you're just getting started or ready to scale, my job is to help you avoid costly mistakes and get consistent results.

I work in person across Kampala, and virtually with hosts wherever they are.

“My goal is simple: to help you build a thriving Airbnb business with confidence and clarity.”
